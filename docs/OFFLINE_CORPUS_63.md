## Held-out custody decision

**Recommended Owner decision:** designate one existing organization-controlled private artifact location and its access policy before the independent corpus team authors the private 60-case bundle. Authorize only the named corpus author and custodian to write, and named oracle reviewer and evaluators to read. Keep runtime/prompt authors outside the ACL and give the future runtime only one scheduled question plus approved source passages. Never include a private location, access credential, plaintext question or oracle in this repo, a PR, or a public log. No existing approved location and access policy were evidenced. No service provisioning is proposed under Issue #63.

This gives the Owner blind scoring and a durable handoff. Cost is unknown until an existing location and its terms are confirmed; incremental spend is outside this approval. If custody leaks or a reviewer loses independence, revoke access, mark the release contaminated, destroy exposed copies under the approved retention policy, and author a fresh release. Store the immutable review decision and access log privately. The Owner need only designate the location and permitted roles; the technical team handles versioning and validation.

After separate custody approval, freeze the canonical private cases array and its byte length plus SHA-256 in the private envelope, using `private_oracle_archive` for the detached canonical `cases` array bytes to avoid self-hashing. Keep raw per-artifact hashes and low-entropy labels private. If a public commitment is needed, use one SHA-256 commitment over a fixed domain prefix, an independent secret 256-bit nonce and the complete canonical private envelope. Publish that commitment with a non-sensitive receipt, keeping the nonce private. Store author, independent reviewer, custodian and distinct future runtime author identities and role assignment privately. Require chair review of all 60 before any freeze. Reject paraphrases, translations, same-item quantity changes, semantic overlap with dev seeds, and label-only distinctions. Compare typed fact/decision/authority families independently across all ten categories. Seal ordered IDs, source digest, exact question/oracle bytes, approval and two scoring seeds before tuning. The public validator checks structural design separation only and never loads private records.
